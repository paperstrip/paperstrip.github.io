Filing, copying, replying: your front desk spends its days on it. What if your tools took care of it?

Custom automations, connected to the software your clinic already uses. Documents go to the right record, information is no longer typed twice from one tool to another, and replies to patients arrive drafted in your inbox. Your team reviews and approves. Your patients' data stays in Europe.

Describe a task in your clinic

Version française

What keeps your team busy every week

Nothing medical, and yet a large part of the front desk's day.

  • The same questions, by email, web form, Instagram or WhatsApp: how a treatment works, aftercare, price ranges, availability.
  • Appointment requests to sort before passing them on to the doctor.
  • Care instructions before and after a treatment, sent again to every patient.
  • Documents in each record (quotes, consent forms, correspondence) to rename and file.
  • Double entry between the calendar, billing and other tools.

What the automations take care of

Inside the tools your clinic already uses. Nothing new to install: your team keeps its habits, and keeps control of everything that goes to a patient.

Patient enquiries, answered in draft

Replies are drafted from information you have approved (how a treatment works, aftercare, price ranges), straight in your inbox. Your team reviews, adjusts and sends.

Appointment requests, sorted

The treatment requested and any missing information are picked out, and the appointment proposal is prepared. Your front desk confirms instead of rebuilding each request.

Instructions and reminders

The before and after care instructions you wrote are prepared for each patient at the right time, together with appointment reminders.

Documents filed

Quotes, consent forms and correspondence are read by OCR, renamed to your convention and filed in the right record. Doubtful cases are set aside for a person.

No more double entry

Your calendar, billing and other tools talk to each other. Information entered once reaches every place it needs to be.

Your figures, without spreadsheets

Requests received, appointments booked, most requested treatments: gathered in one place and queryable in plain language.

What the AI does not do

  • No diagnosis, no medical advice. Whether a treatment is appropriate remains the doctor's decision, made in consultation.
  • Nothing is sent without review. Every reply to a patient is approved by your team.
  • Nothing made up. Only information you have approved is used. Communication about aesthetic procedures is regulated, with rules specific to each country: you decide what is said.
  • Nothing published in your name. Neither on your website nor on your social media.

Your patients' data

Health data: a special category under the GDPR, which applies across the European Union, with medical confidentiality on top. The framework is set before the first line of code, according to your clinic's country.

  • Only what is needed is sent to the AI model to handle a request.
  • European servers, zero retention: models run in Europe, and neither the platform nor the model provider keeps the data or uses it for training.
  • Every operation is logged: what went in, what came out, and when.
  • EU AI Act: an administrative assistant that makes no medical decision is not a high-risk use. If a patient interacts directly with an AI tool, they are told so. This classification is checked during scoping.

How it starts

  • Joint scoping, by video call wherever your clinic is. You list the questions and tasks that come back, with their volume; the questions we ask help pin them down and price them. Scoping is not billed.
  • A first type of request goes live in your tools and is used for real by your team.
  • Then we extend what has proven itself to other tasks.

A first automation starts from €500 excluding VAT. The mechanisms described here already run in production in other sectors. Projects are run in English or French.

Frequently asked questions

Does the AI reply to my patients directly?

No. It drafts the reply from information you have approved, and your team reviews it before sending. It makes no diagnosis and gives no medical advice.

Do I need to change my booking or patient record software?

No. The automations connect to the tools you already use through their API. When a tool cannot be modified, a browser extension adds the suggestions on top of the interface your team already knows.

Is my patients' health data protected?

Health data is a special category under the GDPR and is handled as such: only what is needed is sent to the model, AI models run on European servers with zero-retention providers, nothing is used to train a model, and every operation is logged.

Do you work with clinics outside Belgium?

Yes. Everything is done remotely: scoping by video call, connection to your tools through their API, follow-up online. On-site meetings are possible in Belgium. The rules that apply in your country, for health data and for communication about aesthetic procedures, are checked during scoping.

Have you already worked with aesthetic clinics?

Not yet in this sector. The mechanisms described here already run in production elsewhere: drafted replies for about 10,000 emails a month, and automatic filing of 100 to 200 documents a month for a credit broker. Scoping is precisely where we check what applies to your clinic.

How much does it cost?

A first simple automation, covering one type of request or one type of document, starts from €500 excluding VAT. The price grows with the number of tools to connect and tasks covered. AI model usage is billed on top, by consumption. Every project is quoted before work starts.
